01 / About me

About me

I'm Sanskruti, a forensic psychology graduate who cares about protecting people, whether that means working through a case, supporting someone in crisis, or helping a team do its best work.

I earned my M.A. in Forensic Psychology from George Washington University. My experience includes missing children casework at NCMEC, forensic psychology research, open-source investigations, and crisis support on RAINN's national hotline.

Supervisors and colleagues describe me as approachable and easy to work with. I'm comfortable talking with people from all walks of life, I take confidentiality seriously, and I bring the same care to every task, big or small.

Approachable

People open up to me easily. I connect with people of every background, from survivors in crisis to professionals and colleagues.

Calm under pressure

Crisis hotline work taught me to stay steady, listen closely and think clearly when the stakes are high.

Always learning

I keep building my skills, with 27 certifications completed alongside my degrees and work.

Leader and team player

From student council to president of GW's Forensic Psychology Student Association, I enjoy bringing people together and getting things done.
